# The 90-second demo

One room, one phone, one trip: Paris → Brussels → Amsterdam, 5 days, €900.

## Run 1 — the macro (30s)
1. (Fresh room) The kickoff lands; Butler turns follow-ups off.
2. Human: `@planner plan the trip from the brief`
3. Trip Planner posts the macro plan: 5 days, the Brussels detour card
   ("Add a city. Not a cost."), free-day placement (Louvre on first Sunday).
4. Deal Finder posts the pass grid: Paris BUY IT (4 rides), Brussels skip
   (2 rides), Amsterdam BUY IT (3 rides).
5. Booking Agent: approval card — Anne Frank House, Day 4, +€18.
6. Human (on the phone): "approve". Booking logs it; Budget posts the card:
   342.60 / 900, 38% used.

## Run 2 — on the go (the money shot, 30s)
1. Human voice note: "just finished the Louvre, what's next"
2. Butler relays in two lines; Day Planner re-plans from the current
   position: nearest 3 stops, the day-plan card with the route map and the
   day's money line, transit priced by pass-check.
3. Butler, 40 min before the next timed stop: one line — "Heads up: Orsay
   in 40. Metro 2 stops, pass covers it."

## Evidence to capture (this is the rubric)
- Planning time: the trip is planned in under N minutes, on a phone, with
  zero spreadsheets. (Time it.)
- Every approval: the card + the human "approve" + the ledger line, in order.
- Every number: from the scripts, with prices_as_of quoted.
- The remove-Switch line in the README.

## Cut lines if it runs long
- Drop the pass grid (keep the detour card — it is the hook).
- Drop the voice note; type "I'm done here".
- One city's finds is enough; don't show all three finders.
